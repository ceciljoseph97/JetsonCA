How To Interpret The Rankings

View Ranking Reason
Raw inference only RTX 5090 > RTX 3050 > Jetson Orin Mean live-both inference latency is 13.8 ms on RTX 5090, 36.2 ms on RTX 3050, and 50.2 ms on Orin.
Full live loop Jetson Orin > RTX 5090 ≈ RTX 3050 Total loop mean is 168.3 ms on Orin vs 200.6 ms on RTX 5090 and 201.4 ms on RTX 3050.

So Orin is not the fastest GPU, but it is currently the best full live system in this benchmark set because its radar capture stage is lower on average.

Approximate end-to-end loop rates: Orin 5.94 loops/s, RTX 5090 4.99 loops/s, RTX 3050 4.96 loops/s.
